## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-badge`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. The tone pairs (`--c2-badge__success--background` and `--color`, …) recolour a tone everywhere; `--c2-badge--background` and `--c2-badge--color` override the active tone on one badge, and the shape and typography variables turn the pill into a tag, a chip or a counter.
