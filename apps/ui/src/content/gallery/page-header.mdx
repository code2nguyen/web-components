## Looks

Each card resets the site's global heading styles on its `h1` (`font: inherit`) so the title takes the page header's typography. The title scale, the surface and the divider set the tone: `--c2-page-header__title--font-size`, `--c2-page-header--background-color` and `--c2-page-header--border-bottom`.

**Navigation bar** is the mobile top bar: `sticky large-title` in a scrolling pane. Scroll it: the large title fades as it passes under the bar, whose title is centred between the back link (derived from the breadcrumb) and the primary action. Where CSS scroll-driven animations are supported the fade follows the scroll position; elsewhere the bar fades in once the title has gone.
