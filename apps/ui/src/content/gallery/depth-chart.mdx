## Slots and states

The `legend` slot replaces the built-in legend, `tooltip` the tooltip body (the hovered point reaches `renderTooltip` and the `tooltip-change` event when the content depends on it) and `actions` holds controls pinned to the plot's top-right corner. While `loading` or `error` is set, or when there is no data, the chart shows a built-in message that the `loading`, `error` and `empty` slots replace.
