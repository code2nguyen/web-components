## Presets

Looks for `c2-mind-map`. The eight `__branch-N--color` variables colour the branches, `__topic--tint` sets how much of that colour a first-level topic takes, the `__root`, `__topic` and `__subtopic` variables shape the three levels, `__connector--width` and `--taper` the curves, and `__level--gap` and `__sibling--gap` the spacing. A node's own `color` wins over the palette.
