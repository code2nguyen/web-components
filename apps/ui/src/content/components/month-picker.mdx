`c2-month-picker` picks a month the way a billing page or a report filter does: a year with its twelve months, paging buttons to move through the years, and a title that opens a grid of years to jump further. Its value is `YYYY-MM`, as a native month input gives, `YYYY-Qn` with `granularity="quarter"`, `YYYY-Hn` with `granularity="half-year"`, or `YYYY` with `granularity="year"`. With `fiscal-start` and `stepper` it is the period selector of an accounting report: fiscal quarters, and arrows that step through them.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/month-picker'
```

## Usage

```html tag=UsageBlock,package=@c2n/month-picker
<div data-label="Billing period">
  <c2-month-picker name="period" value="2026-09" max="2026-10"></c2-month-picker>
</div>
<div data-label="Fiscal quarter">
  <c2-month-picker granularity="quarter" fiscal-start="7" value="2027-Q2" stepper></c2-month-picker>
</div>
<div data-label="Year only">
  <c2-month-picker granularity="year" value="2024" min="2018" max="2026"></c2-month-picker>
</div>
```

### Value

`value` is the period picked: the month as `YYYY-MM`, the quarter as `YYYY-Q1` to `YYYY-Q4` with `granularity="quarter"`, the half-year as `YYYY-H1` or `YYYY-H2` with `granularity="half-year"`, the year as `YYYY` with `granularity="year"`; anything else is ignored. A click, or Enter or Space on the focused cell, picks it and fires `input` then `change` (with `detail.value`). Month names follow the page's language. When the granularity changes, a value becomes the wider period that holds it (May becomes Q2, Q2 becomes H1 and then the year), and one that no longer fits a single cell is cleared.

`from` and `to` resolve the value to its first and last day as `YYYY-MM-DD` (`2026-07-01` and `2026-09-30` for `2026-Q3`), which is what a report query wants.

### Fiscal years

`fiscal-start` is the month (1 to 12) the fiscal year starts in. It moves the quarters, half-years and years: with `fiscal-start="7"` the year `2027` (shown as FY2027) runs from July 2026 to June 2027, and `2027-Q1` is July to September 2026. A fiscal year is named after the calendar year it ends in. Months are always calendar months. Each quarter and half-year cell shows the months it covers.

### Stepper

`stepper` adds a bar above the grid with the period picked between two arrows, which step it to the previous or next whole period and fire `input` and `change` like a click. With no value they pick the current period. `stepUp(count)` and `stepDown(count)` do the same from code, without events, as on a native input.

### Limits

`min` and `max`, in any of the value's formats (a bare year also works in month mode, a month in quarter mode), disable the periods outside them, stop the stepper's arrows and stop the paging buttons at the first and last year that has one, as a card-expiry field would with this year and ten years ahead.

### Years

In month mode the title shows the year; pressing it opens a grid of twelve years, and picking one there opens its months without changing the value. With `granularity="year"` the picker shows only the grid of years, and a year is the value.

### In a form

Under a `name`, the form receives the value. `required` asks for one. Inside a `c2-form-field`, the field's label names the picker and its hint and error describe it.

### Keyboard

The months (quarters, half-years) are a grid with one tab stop. The arrow keys move by cell and by row, running on into the previous or next year at the ends; Home and End go to the start and end of a row; Page Up and Page Down change the year (or the page of years); Enter or Space picks. The current month is marked, and a screen reader hears each month with its year.
