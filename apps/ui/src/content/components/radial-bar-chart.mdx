A radial bar chart shows a few measures against their goals as concentric rings: the move, exercise and stand rings of a fitness app, a readiness score, what is spent of each budget. One row is one ring, outermost first; a ring past its goal fills its track and draws a darker second lap. It is drawn by ECharts.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/chart/radial-bar-chart'
```

This chart draws with [ECharts](https://echarts.apache.org) 6, a peer dependency it loads on first draw: `npm install echarts`.

### Data

Rows are plain objects. `label-field` names each ring, the one `c2-chart-series` reads its value, and `max-field` its goal; without it, `max` (100 by default) is every ring's goal. `color-field` gives a ring its own colour; otherwise rings take the chart palette in order.

### Shape

A ring starts at twelve o'clock and runs clockwise; `start-angle` turns the start. `sweep` is how much of the circle a full ring covers: `270` leaves the bottom open, the dial of a budget or a speedometer. Ring thickness and the space between rings are `--c2-chart__ring--width` and `--c2-chart__ring--gap`, and a ring narrows when the chart is too small to keep a hole. The track behind each ring is `--c2-chart__track--color`.

Each ring's name is drawn at its start, in black or white for contrast, and left out when the ring is too short to hold it; `labels="none"` leaves them all out, and `legend="bottom"` lists the rings instead. The `center` slot sits in the hole for a total or an icon.

### Reading it

Hovering a ring shows its value against its goal and the share ("24 / 30 · 80%"); `value-suffix` and `precision` format the values. A click highlights a ring and fades the others. For assistive technology the chart carries the same figures as a list.
