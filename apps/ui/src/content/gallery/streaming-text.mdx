## Presets

Looks for `c2-streaming-text` while it streams. The caret variables (`--c2-streaming-text__caret--content`, `--color`, `--blink-duration`) and the typography variables do most of the work; the pacing variables (`--c2-streaming-text__reveal--max-lag`, `--flush-duration`) change how far the text may trail the stream.
