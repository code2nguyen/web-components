## Presets

The box is one set of variables (`--c2-mask-input--border`, `--c2-mask-input--background`, `--c2-mask-input--border-radius`, `--c2-mask-input--min-height`), with `--c2-mask-input__hover--*`, `__focus--*` and `__error--*` states on top. The text and the mask hint behind it share `--c2-mask-input--font-family`, `--c2-mask-input--letter-spacing` and `--c2-mask-input--font-variant-numeric`; `--c2-mask-input__slot--color` colours the hint.
