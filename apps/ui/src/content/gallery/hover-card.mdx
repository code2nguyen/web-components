## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-hover-card`. Hover the trigger in a card to see it. The card is styled through `--c2-hover-card--*`: `background-color`, `color`, `border`, `border-radius` and `box-shadow` set the surface, `width` and the four `padding-*` variables its size, and `offset`, `transition-duration` and `enter-transform` how it arrives.
