## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-sheet`. Each card shows a trigger; open the sheet to see the look. `side` and `--c2-sheet--size` set the geometry, `--c2-sheet--border-radius`, `--c2-sheet--background` and `--c2-sheet--box-shadow` the panel, and the `__header`, `__body`, `__footer`, `__close` and `__backdrop` groups the rest — the same names `c2-modal` uses.
