## Presets

Looks for status pages, incident reviews and build histories. The baseline state is a wash mixed by
`__segment__baseline--background-mix` (recoloured with `__segment__baseline--color`), and the other states are solid
fills of `__segment--height`. A short baseline height with a taller pill for the exceptions gives the minimal strip;
`__segment--gap` and `__segment--border-radius` split a band into blocks, and `__row--height` with `__label--width`
sets the density.
