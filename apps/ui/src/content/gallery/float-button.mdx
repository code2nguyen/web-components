## Looks

The button and each action have their own colour, size, radius and shadow variables. `--c2-float-button__label--display` turns the circle into an extended button, and `--c2-float-button-action__label--display: none` makes the actions icon-only. Every card holds its button in a `contain: layout` stage; in an app the button is fixed to the viewport.
