`c2-scorecard` is a radio [`c2-choice-matrix`](/web-components/components/choice-matrix) that keeps score: each
criterion is a row answered on the scale in the columns, weighted, and the card totals as you go, with a subtotal per
category and a verdict at the bottom. A critical criterion answered with a failing point fails the whole card, whatever
the total. Interview scorecards, support QA reviews, supplier evaluations, grading rubrics and inspections read this
way.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/choice-matrix/scorecard'
```

The entry registers `c2-scorecard` and `c2-choice-matrix`.

## Usage

```html tag=UsageBlock,package=@c2n/choice-matrix
<div data-label="Interview">
  <c2-scorecard name="interview" label="Interview scorecard" row-header="Criterion"
    columns='[{"value":"1","label":"Strong no","fail":true},{"value":"2","label":"No"},{"value":"3","label":"Yes"},{"value":"4","label":"Strong yes"}]'
    rows='[{"value":"design","label":"System design","group":"Skills","weight":2},{"value":"code","label":"Coding","group":"Skills"},{"value":"comms","label":"Communication","group":"Skills"},{"value":"values","label":"Works with others","group":"Culture","critical":true}]'
    bands='[{"min":75,"label":"Hire","tone":"success"},{"min":50,"label":"Lean hire","tone":"warning"},{"min":0,"label":"No hire","tone":"error"}]'
    value='{"design":"4","code":"3","comms":"3"}'></c2-scorecard>
</div>
<div data-label="Call QA">
  <c2-scorecard name="qa" label="Call review" row-header="Check"
    columns='[{"value":"fail","label":"Fail","points":0,"fail":true},{"value":"pass","label":"Pass","points":1},{"value":"na","label":"N/A","na":true}]'
    rows='[{"value":"greeting","label":"Greets and verifies the caller","critical":true},{"value":"empathy","label":"Acknowledges the problem"},{"value":"resolve","label":"Resolves or sets a next step"},{"value":"upsell","label":"Offers the annual plan"}]'
    value='{"greeting":"pass","empathy":"pass","resolve":"fail","upsell":"na"}'></c2-scorecard>
</div>
```

### The scale

Columns are the points of the scale. A column earns its `points`, else its value read as a number, else its position
(the first is 0). `na` marks a "not applicable" column: a row answered with it counts towards neither total. `fail`
marks a failing answer: a `critical` criterion answered with it fails the card.

### Weights, totals and the verdict

A criterion's `weight` multiplies its points and its possible points, and shows beside its label. The score column
gives each criterion's points out of its possible points, each category header its subtotal, and the summary under
the card the total, the percentage, how many criteria are answered and the verdict: the highest of `bands` whose `min`
(a percentage) the score reaches, or the failed critical criteria.

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
const card = document.querySelector('c2-scorecard')!
card.addEventListener('score-change', (event) => {
  const { points, possible, percent, failed, band } = event.detail
  save({ answers: card.value, percent, verdict: failed.length ? 'fail' : band?.label })
})
```

### Comments and rubrics

A comment field goes under a criterion through its `detail-<row>` slot (`<c2-textarea slot="detail-code">`), or
`renderRowDetail` for every row. `cells` writes what each level means in its cell, which is how a grading rubric
reads: the descriptions are part of each answer's name.
