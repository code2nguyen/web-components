## Drawer styles

Every card is a fixed-height frame with a narrow drawer; the look is a handful of `--c2-side-nav--*` variables on a class. The rows inside are plain links styled by the card.

## Modes and positions

The overlay is kept inside its frame with `--c2-side-nav__over--position: absolute`; leave it `fixed` in an app so it covers the viewport. The rail cards put the toggle in the drawer header and hide labels with a rule on `:not([opened])`.
