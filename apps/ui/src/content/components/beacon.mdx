`c2-beacon` puts a pulsing dot on each element worth a word and opens a short tip when the user clicks it. It is the companion of [`c2-tour`](/web-components/components/tour) for help that does not interrupt: the tips stand alone, in no order, several dots show at once, and nothing opens or takes focus by itself. Each tip is a `c2-tour-step` child, as in a tour: a `target` (a CSS selector), a `heading` and the explanation as its content. The dot sits on the target's top end corner and follows it as the page scrolls; a target that is not rendered or scrolled out of view shows no dot, and one added to the page later gets its dot then.

Clicking a dot opens its tip in the tour's panel, beside a ring around the target, with focus on **Got it**. Got it dismisses the tip: its dot goes away, focus moves to the target, and `dismiss` fires with the tip's index and key. The close button, Escape, a click outside or a second click on the dot close the tip and keep the dot. The panel takes the tour's `--c2-tour--*` variables and parts; the page is not dimmed unless `--c2-beacon__backdrop--color` asks for it. `--c2-beacon__dot--*` styles the dot (`--c2-beacon__dot--translate` moves it off the corner) and `--c2-beacon__pulse--*` its pulse, which stops while the tip is open and for users who prefer reduced motion.

Give the beacon a `storage-key` and the dismissed tips are remembered in `localStorage` as `{ "dismissed": ["tip-export", "2"] }`, so a dot dismissed once does not come back; `dismissed` lists them and `reset()` forgets them. A tip is remembered by its step's `id`, else by its position: give each step an `id` of its own (not its target's) so adding a tip later does not bring the others back. The dots are in the tab order where the `c2-beacon` sits, so place it right after the content it explains.

```html tag=CodeBlock
<c2-beacon storage-key="reports-tips-v1">
  <c2-tour-step id="tip-export" target="#export" heading="Export to CSV">Download any view as a spreadsheet.</c2-tour-step>
  <c2-tour-step id="tip-share" target="#share" heading="Share a link">Anyone with the link sees a read-only copy.</c2-tour-step>
</c2-beacon>
```

## Usage

```html tag=UsageBlock,package=@c2n/tour
<style>
  .usage-beacon-toolbar {
    display: flex;
    gap: 16px;
    padding: 8px;
  }
</style>
<div data-label="New features">
  <div class="usage-beacon-toolbar">
    <c2-button id="usage-beacon-export">Export</c2-button>
    <c2-button id="usage-beacon-share">Share</c2-button>
  </div>
  <c2-beacon client:only="lit">
    <c2-tour-step id="tip-export" target="#usage-beacon-export" heading="Export to CSV">Download any view as a spreadsheet.</c2-tour-step>
    <c2-tour-step id="tip-share" target="#usage-beacon-share" heading="Share a link" placement="right">Anyone with the link sees a read-only copy.</c2-tour-step>
  </c2-beacon>
</div>
```
