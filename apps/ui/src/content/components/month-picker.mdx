`c2-month-picker` picks a month the way a billing page or a report filter does: a year with its twelve months, paging buttons to move through the years, and a title that opens a grid of years to jump further. Its value is `YYYY-MM`, as a native month input gives, or `YYYY` with `granularity="year"`.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/month-picker'
```

## Usage

```html tag=UsageBlock,package=@c2n/month-picker
<div data-label="Billing period">
  <c2-month-picker name="period" value="2026-09" max="2026-10"></c2-month-picker>
</div>
<div data-label="Year only">
  <c2-month-picker granularity="year" value="2024" min="2018" max="2026"></c2-month-picker>
</div>
```

### Value

`value` is the month picked as `YYYY-MM`, or the year as `YYYY` with `granularity="year"`; anything else is ignored. A click, or Enter or Space on the focused month, picks it and fires `input` then `change` (with `detail.value`). Month names follow the page's language.

### Limits

`min` and `max`, in the same format as the value (a bare year also works in month mode), disable the months outside them and stop the paging buttons at the first and last year that has one, as a card-expiry field would with this year and ten years ahead.

### Years

In month mode the title shows the year; pressing it opens a grid of twelve years, and picking one there opens its months without changing the value. With `granularity="year"` the picker shows only the grid of years, and a year is the value.

### In a form

Under a `name`, the form receives the value. `required` asks for one. Inside a `c2-form-field`, the field's label names the picker and its hint and error describe it.

### Keyboard

The months are a grid with one tab stop. The arrow keys move by month and by row, running on into the previous or next year at the ends; Home and End go to the start and end of a row; Page Up and Page Down change the year (or the page of years); Enter or Space picks. The current month is marked, and a screen reader hears each month with its year.
