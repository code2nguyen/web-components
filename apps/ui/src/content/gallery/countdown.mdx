## Presets

From a quiet product-page countdown to a launch hero and a quiz timer. The segment variables (`__segment--background-color`, `__segment--border`, `__segment--min-width`) and the value size do most of the work; `__separator--display` brings the colons in and `__value--animation-duration` rolls each new digit in.
