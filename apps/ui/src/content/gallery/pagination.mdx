## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-pagination`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. `--c2-pagination__item--*` carries
the page numbers and `--c2-pagination__item__selected--*` the current one; `--c2-pagination__nav--*` styles the
previous/next controls on their own, and `--c2-pagination--justify-content` places the row in its container.
