`c2-time-slot-picker` is the booking step of Calendly and Cal.com. A `c2-date-strip` picks the day, with a dot on the days that have a free time, and the day's times show below it, grouped into morning, afternoon and evening. A time that is taken stays in place, struck through.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/time-slot-picker'
```

### Slots

`slots` lists the times, as a JSON attribute or a property: each has a `start`, an ISO date-time with its offset (`2026-11-02T09:00:00+01:00` or `…Z`), an optional `end`, an optional `label` shown in place of the time, and `available: false` for one that is taken. The picker sorts them and files each under its day.

### Time zone

`time-zone` names the IANA zone the times are shown in (`Europe/Paris`); by default it is the browser's. A slot is filed under its day *in that zone*, so 23:00 UTC is the next morning in Tokyo. The line under the days names the zone.

### Days

The day shown is `date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`). By default it is the day of the value, else the first day from today with a free time. Days with a free time carry a dot. Days between the first and last slot with none are disabled, and the arrow keys step over them. On a day with no free time, the picker says so and offers the next day that has one. Showing another day fires `date-change`.

Paging the days fires `range-change` with the first and last day now shown. An app that loads slots a week at a time listens for it, sets `loading` (which shows placeholder times), and sets `slots` when they arrive.

### Value

The value is the picked slot's `start`, exactly as given. A click, or Enter or Space on the focused time, picks it and fires `input` then `change`, whose `detail` holds the value and the slot. `show-end` shows each time with its end, and `group-by="none"` lists the day's times as one group.

### In a form

Under a `name`, the form receives the value. `required` asks for one. Inside a `c2-form-field`, the field's label names the picker and its hint and error describe the times.

### Keyboard

The times have one tab stop. The arrow keys move from time to time across the groups and step over taken ones, Home and End go to the first and last, and Enter or Space picks. The days keep the strip's own keys.
