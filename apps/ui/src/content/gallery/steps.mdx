## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-steps`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. Two variables do most of the work:
`--c2-step__rail--width` turns a trace into a stepper, and `--c2-step__row--border-bottom` turns a stepper back into
a table of rows. Every step is a row and every row is on screen: a sub-step is indented until its marker sits under
its parent's label, which is all the nesting needs. `--c2-step__guide--width: 1px` adds file-tree rules on top of
that, and the `toggle` slot adds a chevron — both off by default.
