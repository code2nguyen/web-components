## Presets

Looks for `c2-pan-zoom`. `--c2-pan-zoom--height` sizes the viewport, the `__controls` and `__button` variables shape the toolbar, the `__minimap` ones the overview, and `__grid--display: block` draws a dot grid that scales with the zoom.
