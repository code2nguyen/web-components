## Presets

Layouts and looks for `c2-json-form`. The structure comes from the `ui-schema` (groups, tabs, stepper, lists); spacing and
the field look come from `--c2-json-form__*` and the `--c2-form-field__*` variables set on the form, which every field
inherits. Open any card in the studio to start from it.

<GalleryGrid columns="2">

  schema='{"type":"object","required":["name"],"properties":{"name":{"type":"string","title":"Full name"},"email":{"type":"string","format":"email"},"newsletter":{"type":"boolean","title":"Send me the newsletter"}}}'
