## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-modal`. Each card shows a trigger; open the dialog to see the look. The box is `--c2-modal--*` (width, margins, radius, shadow, enter transform), the sections are `__header`, `__body` and `__footer`, the × button is `__close`, and the backdrop colour and blur are `__backdrop`.
