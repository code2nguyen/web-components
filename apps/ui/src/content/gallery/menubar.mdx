## Presets

Looks for `c2-menubar`. The bar's frame comes from `--c2-menubar--*` (background, border, radius, padding). The triggers come from `--c2-menubar-menu__trigger--*`, which you can set on the bar because they inherit. The open trigger is `__open`, and the menus underneath take `--c2-menu--*`.
