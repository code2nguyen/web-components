A treemap splits a whole into rectangles whose areas follow their values, grouped the way the data is: stocks by
sector, spending by department, files by folder. Unlike a pie, it holds hundreds of parts and a hierarchy, and the
largest parts stand out at a glance. One row is one tile, named by `label-field` and sized by the single series. It is
drawn by ECharts' treemap series.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/chart/treemap-chart'
```

This chart draws with [ECharts](https://echarts.apache.org) 6, a peer dependency it loads on first draw: `npm install echarts`.

### Data

Rows are plain objects. A row is a tile: `label-field` names it, and the one `c2-chart-series` sizes it. Rows without a
positive value are left out, and so is a group left empty. There are two ways to give the hierarchy:

- **Flat rows with `group-field`.** The field names the group a row belongs to, as a market map groups stocks by
  sector. A comma-separated list nests the groups, outermost first: `group-field="region, sector"`.
- **Nested rows with `children-field`** (`children` by default). A row with children is a group of them and is sized
  by them, as a folder is by its files; children may nest to any depth.

Siblings are laid out largest first. The `nodes` getter returns every group and tile in that order, each with its path,
value, share of the whole and share of its group; `buildTreemap(rows, options)` is the same computation as a plain
function.

### Colour

Without `color-field`, each top-level group takes the next colour of `--c2-chart__series-1…8--color` and its tiles
share it; a group keeps its colour when another is hidden. Each group's name sits in a header strip above its tiles.

With `color-field`, tiles are coloured by that field on a scale around zero instead: from `--c2-chart__neutral--color`
at zero towards `--c2-chart__positive--color` above it and `--c2-chart__negative--color` below, reaching full strength
at `color-max` (by default the largest value either side). `color-label` names the measure in the tooltip and the
table, and `color-suffix` follows its value, or `formatColor` formats it from code. Text on a tile is white or
near-black, whichever reads better on it.

`--c2-chart__tile--gap`, `--c2-chart__tile--border-radius` and `--c2-chart__group--gap` set the spacing.

### Interaction

The tooltip gives a tile's path, its value, its `color-field` value, and its share of the whole and of its group;
hovering a group header describes the whole group. `point-click` reports the tile or group, and clicking one
highlights its top-level group, dimming the rest, as a legend entry does.

The headers already name every group, so the legend is off by default. With `legend="bottom"` it lists the top-level
groups; with `legend-action="toggle"` an entry hides its group (`setGroupVisible(name, false)` does the same from code)
and the rest grow into the space. A visually hidden table lists every tile with its group, value and share for screen
readers, and names the plot.
