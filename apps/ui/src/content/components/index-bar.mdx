`c2-index-bar` is the rail of letters beside a contact list, a glossary or any long list cut into sections. It is a [`c2-table-of-contents`](/web-components/components/table-of-contents) in another shape: it reads the headings of the element named by `for` (with any `selector`), files each section under the first character of its label, marks the key of the section being read and jumps when a key is tapped. Dragging along the rail scrubs through the list, section by section, while a bubble beside the rail shows the key under the pointer.

`keys` sets the alphabet the rail always shows (`ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ#`): a key no section is filed under is dimmed, dragging over it moves on to the next key that has one, and a section whose key is not listed is filed under the key that differs only by accents (`É` under `E`), else under `#`. Without `keys` the rail lists the keys of the sections. The rail is a single Tab stop: the arrow keys, Home and End walk its keys and Enter jumps, moving focus to the section's heading as a click does.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/table-of-contents/index-bar'
```

The rail reads the `h5` headings of the list next to it (`for="ib-usage-list" selector="h5"`). Each heading needs an `id`; its text, or its `data-toc-label`, gives the key. `keep-url` leaves the address alone, which suits a list inside an app; without it a jump records the section in the URL fragment, as a link would.

## Sections given as items

When the sections are known in advance, hand them over as `items`, each with the `id` of its heading and, when its label does not start with it, the `key` it is filed under:

```html tag=CodeBlock
<c2-index-bar keys="ABC" items='[{"id":"a","label":"Ada","key":"A"},{"id":"c","label":"Carl"}]'></c2-index-bar>
```

## Placing the rail

The rail is as tall as its keys. Place it beside the list in a flex row (or in a sticky column of the page) and centre it vertically; the bubble sits towards the list, `--c2-index-bar__bubble--inset-inline-end` from the rail's end edge, so leave it room or set `--c2-index-bar__bubble--display: none`. `offset` works as on the table of contents: set it to the height of a sticky header above the list.
