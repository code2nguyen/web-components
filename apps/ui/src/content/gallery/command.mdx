## Presets

`--c2-command--*` is the surface (background, a border per side, a radius per corner, shadow), `--c2-command__field--*` the search row and `--c2-command__list--*` the scrolling list. The rows are themed through `--c2-command-item--*`, most visibly `--c2-command-item__active--background` for the highlight.
