Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Institutional Commitment

AxiomData AI Technologies Ltd ("AxiomData", "we", "our") is committed to safeguarding personal and business data entrusted to us by institutional clients, partners, and website visitors. We operate under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and international data protection standards.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information provided directly by you when requesting access, downloading documentation, or entering contractual agreements: full name, business email address, corporate telephone number, job title, institutional firm name, and API access logs.

3. Use of Information

We process institutional information strictly for: delivering subscribed data feeds and analytics, administering API access and security tokens, ensuring compliance with market data dissemination rules, providing technical support, and fulfilling contractual legal obligations.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted to or from AxiomData endpoints is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest utilizing AES-256 encryption. We enforce zero-trust access controls, regular third-party penetration audits, and SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure.
