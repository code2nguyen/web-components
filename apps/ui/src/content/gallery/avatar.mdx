## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-avatar`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. `--c2-avatar--size` sets the box and the font follows; `--c2-avatar--box-shadow` draws rings without touching layout; the `auto-color` hue is tuned with the `__auto-color` tokens and the dot or slotted badge with `__badge`.
