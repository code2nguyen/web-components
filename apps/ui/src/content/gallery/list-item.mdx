## Navigation

`current` marks the row of the page the reader is on (`aria-current="page"`); it takes the selected look unless the `--c2-list-item__current--*` variables say otherwise. `c2-list-group` folds rows under a heading with an icon and a count, and groups nest. Put the rows in a `<nav>`, not in a `c2-list`: they are links, not options.

## Settings rows

A control in the `control` slot turns the row into a settings row: the row's text names the control, its `description` describes it, and a click anywhere on the row flips a switch or checkbox or focuses a select. The control keeps its own role, focus and keys. For a row that opens another page with its current value, use an `href` row with the value and a chevron in `suffix-icon`.
