A sunburst draws a hierarchy as rings: the centre is the whole, the first ring splits it into its top-level branches, and
each ring further out splits its parent, the way disk-usage tools show folders and a budget breaks down by department,
team and line item. Where a treemap packs many parts into rectangles, a sunburst shows the levels themselves, and clicking
a branch zooms into it. One row is one node, named by `label-field` and sized by the single series. It is drawn by
ECharts' sunburst series.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/chart/sunburst-chart'
```

This chart draws with [ECharts](https://echarts.apache.org) 6, a peer dependency it loads on first draw: `npm install echarts`.

### Data

Rows are read the [treemap's](../treemap-chart) way. A row is a node: `label-field` names it, and the one
`c2-chart-series` sizes it. Rows without a positive value are left out, and so is a group left empty.

- **Nested rows with `children-field`** (`children` by default). A row with children is a group of them and is sized
  by them, as a folder is by its files; children may nest to any depth.
- **Flat rows with `group-field`.** The field names the group a row belongs to; a comma-separated list nests the groups,
  outermost first: `group-field="region, sector"`.

Siblings run clockwise from twelve o'clock, largest first. The `nodes` getter returns every group and leaf in that
order, each with its path, value, share of the whole and share of its group.

### Rings and colour

`levels` (3 by default) caps how many rings show from the current centre; deeper levels come into view as the reader
zooms, and a branch with fewer levels spreads them over the whole radius. Each top-level branch takes the next colour of
`--c2-chart__series-1…8--color`, and below it siblings step lighter toward the surface, largest darkest, so a zoomed
branch is never one flat colour. A node keeps its colour when it is zoomed into view.

With `color-field`, every segment is coloured by that field on the treemap's scale around zero instead, from
`--c2-chart__neutral--color` toward `--c2-chart__positive--color` and `--c2-chart__negative--color`, at full strength at
`color-max`; a group takes the value-weighted mean of its leaves.

Names are drawn where they fit (`labels="auto"`): along the arc on the first ring and on wide arcs, along the radius on
narrow, deep ones, never upside down. A segment too small for its name stays blank, and the tooltip still names it;
`labels="none"` hides them all. The hole shows the current centre's name ("Total" at the top) and value, and the
`center` slot replaces it. `value-prefix` and `value-suffix` frame every value, or a series' `format` formats it.

`--c2-chart__segment--gap`, `--c2-chart__segment--border-radius` and `--c2-chart__hole--size` (a share of the radius)
set the shape.

### Zoom

Clicking a group makes it the centre: its children form the first ring, a breadcrumb ("All › Engineering") appears top
left, and the hole becomes a "Back to All" button that goes up one level. `zoom-change` fires first with the new
`path` and `node`; it is cancelable, so an app can load a branch before showing it. `root` holds the zoom as a path
of names (an attribute takes it as JSON, and a server renders the breadcrumb from it), and `zoomTo(path)` and
`zoomOut()` drive it from code. `breadcrumb="none"` hides the breadcrumb. The arcs morph into the new layout in 400 ms,
or at once under reduced motion or `animation="none"`.

Clicking a leaf fires `point-click` and highlights it, dimming everything off its path, as on the other charts.

### Interaction and accessibility

Hovering a segment keeps its path to the centre and fades everything else to `--c2-chart__series__dimmed--opacity`.
The tooltip gives the path, the value and its shares of the whole and of its group.

The plot is one tab stop. The left and right arrows move around a ring between siblings, down goes to the first child
and up to the parent; Enter zooms into a group (or clicks a leaf), and Escape or Backspace zooms out. The focused
segment gets a ring in `--c2-chart__segment__focus--color`, and a polite announcement reads it ("Sales › Field, $1.6M,
13.7% of total, 2 items"). The plot is named by a summary, and a visually hidden table lists every leaf with its path,
value and shares.

The labels already name the branches, so the legend is off by default; `legend="bottom"` lists the top-level branches
for a small sunburst without labels, and with `legend-action="toggle"` an entry hides its branch
(`setGroupVisible(name, false)` does the same from code).
