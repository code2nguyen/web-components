## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-split-panel`. The divider is hidden until the pointer finds it (or it is dragged or keyboard-focused); `--c2-split-panel__divider--opacity: 1` keeps it on screen, as the thick bar, accent grip and dark looks do. The `__divider` group does most of the work — `--c2-split-panel__divider--size` is the room the line takes, `--hit-area` the grab area around it, and `--background` with its `__hover` and `__active` states the colour — while `--c2-split-panel__handle--display: none` removes the grip and `__start` / `__end` colour the panels.
