## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-tree`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. The container is `--c2-tree--*` (background, padding, borders, radius, `max-height`); every row is `--c2-tree-item--*`, where `row--indent` and `row--border-radius` do most of the work — set `row--border-radius` to `0` for the edge-to-edge bands of a layers panel, and `actions--opacity` to `1` to stop the trailing actions hiding until hover. Add `children-outline` for the indent rules, then colour them with `guide--color`.
