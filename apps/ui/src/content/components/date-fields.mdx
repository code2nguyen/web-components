`c2-date-fields` is the companion of [`c2-date-input`](/web-components/components/date-input) for dates people know by
heart: a date of birth, the issue date on a passport, the day a policy started. Typing three short numbers is quicker
there than paging a calendar back forty years, so it draws a day, a month and a year field with their labels, in the
order of the page's language (month first in American English, day first in British English and French). It is one
form control: its `value` is the ISO date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) once the three parts make a real one, and empty until then.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/date-input/date-fields'
```

The entry registers `c2-date-fields` only. It loads none of the date input's picker or calendar code.

## Usage

```html tag=UsageBlock,package=@c2n/date-input
<div data-label="Date of birth">
  <c2-date-fields aria-label="Date of birth" autocomplete="bday" help="For example, 27 3 2007" lang="en-GB"></c2-date-fields>
</div>
<div data-label="Order and bounds">
  <c2-date-fields aria-label="Passport issued" order="ymd" value="2019-06-14" min="2010-01-01" max="2026-12-31"></c2-date-fields>
</div>
<div data-label="Errors">
  <c2-date-fields aria-label="Start date" lang="en-GB" value="2026-02-30" error></c2-date-fields>
  <c2-date-fields aria-label="Renewal" value="2027-03-01" readonly></c2-date-fields>
</div>
```

The fields take digits on a numeric keyboard (`inputmode="numeric"`), never move focus on their own (Tab goes from one
to the next) and accept a day or month without its leading zero. `order` fixes the order with the letters `d`, `m` and
`y`; `autocomplete="bday"` gives the fields `bday-day`, `bday-month` and `bday-year`, which browsers fill from a saved
birth date.

Validation names the part that is wrong and marks that field: "Date must include a month", "Day must be a number from 1
to 31", "Year must have 4 digits", "Date must be a real date" (31 April), then `min` and `max` written out in the page's
language. The message replaces `help` once focus leaves a group the user has typed in, or when the form is submitted;
`error` shows it at once, or `error-text` in its place. The validity is a custom error, so a
[`c2-form-field`](/web-components/components/form-field) around the fields shows the same message under its own label:

```html tag=CodeBlock
<c2-form-field label="Date of birth" hint="For example, 27 3 2007" validate-on="blur">
  <c2-date-fields name="dob" autocomplete="bday" required></c2-date-fields>
</c2-form-field>
```

The labels and messages are `date-fields` message tokens and follow the nearest `lang`:

```ts tag=CodeBlock
import { addTranslations } from '@c2n/core/localize.js'

addTranslations({
  fr: {
    'date-fields': {
      day: 'Jour',
      month: 'Mois',
      year: 'Année',
      missingMonth: 'La date doit comporter un mois',
      badMonth: 'Le mois doit être un nombre de 1 à 12',
      min: 'La date doit être le {0} ou après',
    },
  },
})
```
