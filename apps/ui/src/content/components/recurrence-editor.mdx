`c2-recurrence-editor` is the custom recurrence form of Google Calendar and Outlook, of a repeating invoice or a maintenance schedule: pick a frequency, an interval ("every 2 weeks"), the weekdays, the day of the month or "the third Tuesday", the month of a yearly rule, and how it ends (never, on a date, after N occurrences). A sentence sums the rule up and the next occurrences show under it.

`value` is an RFC 5545 RRULE (`FREQ=WEEKLY;INTERVAL=2;BYDAY=MO,WE;COUNT=10`). With `format="cron"` it is a five-field cron expression instead (`30 9 * * 1-5`), the schedule of a job: the editor then offers only what cron can say (a time of day, steps that divide their range, no end). `start` is the first occurrence, which seeds the weekday, the day and the month when the frequency changes; `frequencies` lists the frequencies offered (add `none` for "Does not repeat", an empty value); `with-time` adds a time-of-day row. It is a form control: the form receives `value` under `name`. The same rule is always available as `rule` (an object), `toRRule()` and `cron`, and `parseRRule`, `formatRRule`, `parseCron`, `formatCron`, `occurrences` and `describeRecurrence` are exported for tables and servers.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/recurrence-editor'
```

## Usage

```html tag=UsageBlock,package=@c2n/recurrence-editor
<style>
  .recurrence-width {
    width: min(100%, 520px);
  }
</style>
<div data-label="Weekly">
  <c2-recurrence-editor class="recurrence-width" start="2026-11-16" value="FREQ=WEEKLY;INTERVAL=2;BYDAY=MO,WE;COUNT=10"></c2-recurrence-editor>
</div>
<div data-label="Monthly">
  <c2-recurrence-editor class="recurrence-width" start="2026-11-17" value="FREQ=MONTHLY;BYDAY=3TU;UNTIL=20271231" preview-count="3"></c2-recurrence-editor>
</div>
<div data-label="Cron">
  <c2-recurrence-editor class="recurrence-width" format="cron" start="2026-11-16" value="30 9 * * 1-5" preview-count="3"></c2-recurrence-editor>
</div>
<div data-label="Calendar event">
  <c2-recurrence-editor class="recurrence-width" frequencies="none daily weekly monthly yearly" with-time start="2026-11-16T10:00" preview-count="0"></c2-recurrence-editor>
</div>
<div data-label="Disabled">
  <c2-recurrence-editor class="recurrence-width" start="2026-11-16" value="FREQ=DAILY" preview-count="0" disabled></c2-recurrence-editor>
</div>
```

Next occurrences count from `start` (today when it is not set) in wall-clock time: a rule repeats "at 09:00" wherever it is read. RRULE parts the editor does not edit (`BYYEARDAY`, several `BYMONTHDAY`s) are kept as written, and the next occurrences are then hidden because they would ignore them. Inside a `c2-form-field`, the field's label names the editor's group.
