`c2-video-player` puts themed controls over a real `<video>`, the way Loom and YouTube draw them: the seek bar split into chapters, a thumbnail while you scrub, captions, speed, picture-in-picture and full screen. The video stays yours, with its sources and caption tracks, so every native feature keeps working.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/video-player'
```

## Usage

```html tag=UsageBlock,package=@c2n/video-player
<style>
  .video-demo {
    width: 100%;
    max-width: 560px;
  }
</style>
<div data-label="Chapters, captions, thumbnails">
  <c2-video-player class="video-demo" label="Product tour" thumbnails="/web-components/media/tour-thumbs.vtt">
    <video src="/web-components/media/tour.webm" poster="/web-components/media/tour-poster.jpg" preload="metadata" playsinline>
      <track kind="captions" srclang="en" label="English" src="/web-components/media/tour.en.vtt" />
      <track kind="captions" srclang="fr" label="Français" src="/web-components/media/tour.fr.vtt" />
      <track kind="chapters" srclang="en" src="/web-components/media/tour.chapters.vtt" />
    </video>
  </c2-video-player>
</div>
<div data-label="A single file">
  <c2-video-player
    class="video-demo"
    label="Release notes"
    src="/web-components/media/tour.webm"
    poster="/web-components/media/tour-poster.jpg"
    chapters='[{"start":0,"title":"What’s new"},{"start":12,"title":"Coming next"}]'
  ></c2-video-player>
</div>
```

### The video

Put a `<video>` inside the player, with its `<source>` and `<track>` children, and leave its `controls` off. The player draws the controls and never writes an attribute on the video, so several sources, captions, `crossorigin` and an HLS or DASH library attached to the element all keep working. For a single file, `src` and `poster` render the player's own video. `media` gives the video element, for example to hand to a `c2-transcript`:

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
const player = document.querySelector('c2-video-player')!
document.querySelector('c2-transcript')!.media = player.media
```

### Chapters and thumbnails

The seek bar shows the buffered range and the played part, split into one segment per chapter. Chapters come from `chapters` (`[{ "start": 0, "title": "Intro" }, …]`) or from a `<track kind="chapters">`; `chapter-change` fires as playback enters one, and the time shows the chapter's title.

Hovering or dragging over the bar shows the time and the chapter. With `thumbnails` it also shows a frame: give it the URL of a WebVTT file whose cues name an image or a region of a sprite, the format most video platforms export:

```text tag=CodeBlock
WEBVTT

00:00.000 --> 00:02.000
tour-thumbs.jpg#xywh=0,0,160,90
```

Or set it to a function of the time that returns a URL or `{ url, x, y, w, h }`.

### Controls

Play and pause, the time, volume with mute, the captions button (it turns the last track on and off), a settings menu with the speed and the caption track, picture-in-picture where the browser has it, and full screen, which covers the whole player so the controls stay. While the video plays, the controls hide 2.5 s after the pointer stops and come back on any movement, on focus and on pause. A big play button covers the poster until the first play, a spinner shows while the video waits for data, and an error panel says what went wrong, with a button to try again. Below 420 px the volume slider, the chapter name and picture-in-picture give way.

Captions are the browser's own: the player turns the text tracks on and off and the video draws the cues.

### Keyboard and accessibility

While focus is in the player: Space or K plays and pauses, ← and → seek 5 s, J and L seek 10 s, ↑ and ↓ change the volume, M mutes, C toggles captions, F toggles full screen, 0–9 jump to that tenth of the video, Home and End go to the start and the end. The seek bar is a slider read as "1:23 of 4:56, Dashboards", and every control is a button named by what it does. The host is a group named by `label`, and matches `:state(playing)`, `:state(fullscreen)` and `:state(idle)` while the controls are hidden.
