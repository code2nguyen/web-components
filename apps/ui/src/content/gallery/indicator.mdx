## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-indicator`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. The tone pairs (`--c2-indicator__danger--background` and `--color`, …) recolour a tone everywhere, `--c2-indicator--offset-x`/`-y` place the marker on a round target, and the size, ring and typography variables turn it from a dot into a counter or a tag.
