## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-table`. Open any one in the studio to start from it. The surface is `--c2-table--*` (background, borders, radius, shadow), the header is `--c2-table__header--*` and `--c2-table__header-cell--*`, the body is `--c2-table__row--*` (plus the `__hover`, `__selected` and `__odd` states) and `--c2-table__cell--*`, and `--c2-table__sort-icon--*` / `--c2-table__pinned-start--box-shadow` cover the affordances.
