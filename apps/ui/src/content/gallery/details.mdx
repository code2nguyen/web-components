## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-details`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. The frame is `--c2-details--*`, the header is `--c2-details__header--*` with `__icon`, `__content` (the second header row), `__hover`, `__open`, `__focus` and `__disabled` states, and the body is `--c2-details__content--*`. Panels that share a `name` form an exclusive accordion.
