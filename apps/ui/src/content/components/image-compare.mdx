`c2-image-compare` lays two layers on top of each other and cuts the after layer at a divider the reader drags: a retouched photo, a redesign, two versions of a chart or two map layers. The layers are any elements you slot, not only images.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/image-compare'
```

## Usage

```html tag=UsageBlock,package=@c2n/image-compare
<style>
  .compare-demo {
    width: 480px;
    max-width: 100%;
  }
</style>
<div data-label="Before and after">
  <c2-image-compare class="compare-demo" before-label="Before" after-label="After">
    <img slot="before" src="/web-components/images/compare-before.svg" alt="Living room before the makeover" width="800" height="500" />
    <img slot="after" src="/web-components/images/compare-after.svg" alt="Living room after the makeover" width="800" height="500" />
  </c2-image-compare>
</div>
<div data-label="Vertical">
  <c2-image-compare class="compare-demo" orientation="vertical" position="40" before-label="Before" after-label="After">
    <img slot="before" src="/web-components/images/compare-before.svg" alt="Living room before the makeover" width="800" height="500" />
    <img slot="after" src="/web-components/images/compare-after.svg" alt="Living room after the makeover" width="800" height="500" />
  </c2-image-compare>
</div>
<div data-label="Hover">
  <c2-image-compare class="compare-demo" hover label="Makeover, before and after">
    <img slot="before" src="/web-components/images/compare-before.svg" alt="" width="800" height="500" />
    <img slot="after" src="/web-components/images/compare-after.svg" alt="" width="800" height="500" />
  </c2-image-compare>
</div>
```

## Layers

The `before` slot sets the size: give its image a `width` and `height` (or the component a `--c2-image-compare--aspect-ratio`) so the space is reserved before it loads. The `after` slot is laid over it and cut at the divider. Any element works in either slot: a `c2-image`, a `<canvas>`, a chart or a map; it is sized to fill the frame.

`position` is the share of the before layer shown, from 0 to 100 (default 50). `orientation="vertical"` puts the before layer on top. `before-label` and `after-label` draw chips in the corners; each belongs to its layer, so the divider cuts it as it passes.

## Moving the divider

Drag the handle, or press anywhere on the images to move the divider there. With `hover` it follows the pointer without a press (a touch still drags). `input` fires while it moves and `change` once it settles: on release, after a key, or when the pointer leaves in `hover` mode. Read `position` in either.

## Accessibility

The handle is a `slider` whose value reads "30% before, 70% after". Its name is `label`, or "Compare: …" built from the alternative text of the two layers; set `label` when the images are decorative (`alt=""`). The arrow keys move the divider 1% in the direction pressed, Page Up and Page Down 10%, Home and End to the edges.
