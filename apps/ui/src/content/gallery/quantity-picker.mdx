## Presets

Looks for `c2-quantity-picker`. The picker styles the trigger, the panel and the dividers; each `c2-quantity` styles its stepper, and its variables can be set on the picker.

<GalleryGrid min="340">

```html tag=MdxCodeBlock,label=Default

  <c2-quantity name="adults" value="2" min="1" singular="adult" plural="adults">Adults<span slot="hint">Ages 13 or above</span></c2-quantity>
  <c2-quantity name="children" singular="child" plural="children">Children<span slot="hint">Ages 2–12</span></c2-quantity>
</c2-quantity-picker>
```
