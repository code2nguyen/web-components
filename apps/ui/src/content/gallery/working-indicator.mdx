## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-working-indicator`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. The indicator and label colours, `--c2-working-indicator__label--highlight-color` and the animation durations do most of the work; the `indicator`, `effect` and `ellipsis` attributes pick the motion.
