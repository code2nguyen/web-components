## Presets

Looks for `c2-index-bar`. The keys (`--c2-index-bar__key--*`), the current key (`__key__active--*`), the rail behind them (`__keys--*`) and the bubble that follows a drag (`__bubble--*`) do most of the work. Each card lists a short contact list beside its rail: tap a key, or press on the rail and drag.
