## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-carousel`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. `--c2-carousel__slide--width` and
`--c2-carousel--gap` decide how many slides share the viewport, `--c2-carousel__control--*` styles the floating
previous/next buttons and `--c2-carousel__indicator--*` / `--c2-carousel__indicator__selected--*` the dots below.
