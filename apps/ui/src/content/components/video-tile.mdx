`c2-video-tile` draws one participant of a call the way Meet, Zoom and Teams do: their video, or their avatar while the camera is off, a name pill with a live microphone meter, and badges for a raised hand, a pin and a weak connection. It connects to nothing: your call SDK supplies the stream and the state, and the tile draws them.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/video-tile'
```

## Usage


### Video

Set `stream` to the participant's `MediaStream`. It plays in an inner `<video>` that is always muted: the call's audio is yours to play, as with every call SDK, and playing it twice echoes. An SDK that attaches its own `<video>` element (LiveKit's `track.attach()`) puts it in the default slot instead.

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
const tile = document.createElement('c2-video-tile')
tile.name = participant.name
tile.stream = new MediaStream([participant.videoTrack, participant.audioTrack])
tile.muted = !participant.audioEnabled
```

Without a live, enabled video track, or with `video-off`, the tile shows a `c2-avatar` of `avatar-src` or the name's initials. `local` mirrors your own camera and adds "(You)" to the name; `kind="screen"` shows a screen share, fitted inside the tile, never mirrored, and named "Ana Ruiz's screen".

### State

- **Microphone.** The name pill ends with a three-bar `c2-audio-visualizer` of the stream's audio, or of `level` when your SDK reports one. `muted` replaces it with a struck-through microphone.
- **Speaking.** `speaking` draws the accent outline: set it from your SDK's active speaker. Or set `detect-speaking`, and the tile's own meter decides.
- **Badges.** `hand-raised`, `pinned` and a `fair` or `poor` `connection` add their badges. The `badge` slot adds your own (recording, host).
- **Actions.** The `actions` slot shows on hover and focus, top right, for a `c2-menu` of tile actions such as "Pin" or "Mute for everyone".

### Accessibility

The tile is a group named by the participant and their state: "Ana Ruiz, muted, hand raised". Speaking is not part of the name, since it changes several times a second. A hand raised after the tile appeared is announced once.
