## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-popconfirm`. Click the trigger in a card to open it. The bubble is styled like `c2-tooltip`, through `--c2-popconfirm--*` (`background-color`, `color`, `border`, `border-radius`, `box-shadow`, `max-width` and the four `padding-*` variables). `--c2-popconfirm__arrow--size` sizes the arrow (`0px` removes it), `--c2-popconfirm__confirm--*` and `--c2-popconfirm__cancel--*` style the buttons, and `--c2-popconfirm__icon--*` the icon.
