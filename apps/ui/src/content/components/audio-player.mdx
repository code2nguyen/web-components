`c2-audio-player` plays a voice note or a recording the way Slack clips and WhatsApp voice messages do: a play button, the waveform as the seek bar, the time and a speed toggle. It plays `src` with an `<audio>` of its own, or an `<audio>` you put inside it (with its `<source>`s), which it never writes on. `media` is that element either way, to hand to `c2-transcript` or `c2-audio-visualizer`.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/audio-player'
```

## Waveform

`peaks` is the waveform: numbers from 0 to 1, usually computed once when the recording is saved and stored with it. The player brings them to `bars` bars (48 by default), or fewer when the waveform is narrower than that many bars at the bar width and gap. Without `peaks`, the player fetches the file once and decodes it with Web Audio to compute them, which only works for a file it may read (same-origin, or served with CORS); otherwise the bars stay flat. `preload="none"` delays that, and any loading, until the first play.

`duration` (in seconds) shows the length before the audio's metadata has loaded, so a conversation full of voice notes shows every length without fetching every file.

## Playback

The waveform is the seek bar: click or drag it, or focus it and use the arrow keys (5 seconds), Home and End; Space plays and pauses. The played bars take the accent colour. The speed button cycles 1×, 1.5× and 2×. `exclusive` pauses any other `exclusive` player on the page when this one starts, as a chat does. `play()` and `pause()` drive it from script, and the host fires `play`, `pause`, `ended` and `ratechange`.

## Transcript and layouts

`transcript` (text), or richer content in the `transcript` slot (a `c2-transcript` given this player's `media`), adds a toggle that opens the transcript below the player. `variant="bubble"` is the compact chat look: the time and speed under the waveform, at most `--c2-audio-player__bubble--max-width` wide, with the `avatar` slot before the play button.

## Accessibility

The player is a group named by `label`, and the play button says what it does ("Play voice note from Ana", "Pause voice note from Ana"). The waveform is a slider read as "0:12 of 0:47"; the speed and the transcript toggle are buttons, the toggle with its expanded state. A file that cannot play says so in place of the time: the play and speed buttons are disabled, and the waveform is marked disabled and leaves the tab order.
