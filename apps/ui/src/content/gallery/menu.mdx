## Surface

Each card shows a trigger; open the menu to see the look. `--c2-menu--*` is the surface — background, a border per side, a radius per corner, shadow, padding, `min-width`, `max-height` — and `--c2-menu__separator--*` / `--c2-menu__heading--*` the dividers and group headings.

## Rows

The rows are your own `c2-menu-item` children, so every `--c2-menu-item--*` variable set on the menu cascades into all of them: `__hover` and `__focus` states, `__destructive` for delete-style commands, and the `__description`, `__shortcut`, `__icon` and `__indicator` parts.
