## Presets

The pill is one set of `--c2-chip__container--*` variables (border, background, colour, radius, height, padding), with `__hover` and `__selected` states on top. `--c2-chip__icon--*` sizes the prefix and the check mark, and `--c2-chip__remove-button--*` styles the cross.
