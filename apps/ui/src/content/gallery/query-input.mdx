## Presets

The box is one set of variables (`--c2-query-input--border`, `--c2-query-input--background`, `--c2-query-input--border-radius`, `--c2-query-input--font-family`), with `__hover--*` and `__focus--*` states on top. The syntax colours are `--c2-query-input__key--*`, `__value--*`, `__comparator--color`, `__operator--color`, `__negation--color` and `__invalid--color`; each `key:value` term sits on a chip styled by `--c2-query-input__term--*` (background, radius, outset), and `--c2-query-input--word-spacing` leaves room between chips. The suggestions list follows `--c2-query-input__panel--*` and `--c2-query-input__option--*`.
