## Presets

Looks for `c2-table-of-contents`. The rail (`--c2-table-of-contents__track--*`), the sliding indicator (`__indicator--*`) and the current link (`__link__active--*`) do most of the work; `--c2-table-of-contents__marker--content` numbers the entries and `__progress--background-color` fills the rail as reading progress. Each card lists the sections of an imaginary page, so the first entry is current.
