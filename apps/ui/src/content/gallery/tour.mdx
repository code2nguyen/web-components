## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-tour`. Click the button in a card to start its tour. The panel is styled through `--c2-tour--*` (`background-color`, `color`, `border`, `border-radius`, `box-shadow`, `padding`, `width`), the page dimming through `--c2-tour__backdrop--color` and the cut-out through `--c2-tour__spotlight--*`. `--c2-tour__next--*` and `--c2-tour__previous--*` style the buttons.
