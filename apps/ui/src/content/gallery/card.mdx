## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-card`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. The surface is `--c2-card--*` (padding, radius, borders, background, color, shadow), the sections are `--c2-card__header--*`, `--c2-card__footer--*` and `--c2-card__media--*`, and `--c2-card__hover--*` / `--c2-card__focus--*` / `--c2-card__disabled--*` cover the states.
