## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-popconfirm`. Click the trigger in a card to open it. The surface is styled through `--c2-popconfirm--*` (`background-color`, `border`, `border-radius`, `box-shadow`, `max-width` and the four `padding-*` variables). The buttons are styled through `--c2-popconfirm__confirm--*` and `--c2-popconfirm__cancel--*`, and the icon through `--c2-popconfirm__icon--*`.
