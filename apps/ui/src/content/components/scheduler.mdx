`c2-scheduler` lays bookings out per resource: a row for each person, room or machine, time across the top, each
booking a bar from its start to its end. Bookings that overlap on one resource stack in lanes and the row grows to
fit. Resources that share a `group` sit under a group row that collapses. The header pages through the range and
switches between the `day`, `week` and `month` views. With `editable`, users drag to create, move and resize
bookings; the scheduler reports each request and your app updates `events`. `c2-week-planner` stays the one-person
week, and `c2-gantt` the read-only project plan.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/scheduler'
```

## Resources and events

`resources` is an array of `{ id, label, group?, description?, avatar?, color? }`. A `description` adds a second line
under the label, `avatar` an image (the label's initials otherwise), and `color` tints the avatar and the resource's
bars. Ungrouped resources come first, then each group in the order it first appears.

`events` is an array of `{ id, resource, start, end, title, description?, color?, readonly?, pending? }`. `start` and `end` are
wall-clock `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm` strings, the shape `c2-date-time-picker` uses, and every time the scheduler reports
comes back in that shape. A bar takes its `color`, else its resource's, else `--c2-scheduler__event--color`. Both
properties accept JSON as an attribute.

## Absences, holidays and a summary row

Three drawing options turn the scheduler into a team absence calendar:

- **Pending.** An event with `pending: true` is drawn hatched with a dashed edge (a request still to approve), and its
  name ends with "pending". `--c2-scheduler__event__pending--stripe-color` sets the hatching.
- **Bands.** `bands` is an array of `{ start, end, label?, color?, resource? }` shaded under the bars: public holidays,
  a shutdown week, or with `resource` one person's non-working days. A band across every row also shades the header,
  where its `label` is the tooltip, and the slot cursor reads it. The weekend stays shaded as before.
- **Summary row.** `renderSummary` draws a row under the grid with one cell per column. It is called with
  `{ start, end, index, events, resources }`: the column's range and position, the events over it and every resource, and returns what to
  draw ("2 out", a coverage warning). `summary-heading` names the row. Give the content a `part` to style it.

## Views and range

- `view`: `day` draws one column per `slot-minutes` (60 by default); `week` and `month` draw one column per day.
- `date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) is a day of the range shown, today by default. The week starts on the locale's first day.
- `day-start` and `day-end` bound the hours drawn each day; `scroll-time` (`08:00`) is where the day view opens.
- The day view shades the hours outside `working-hours` (`09:00-18:00`); every view shades the locale's weekend.
- A line marks the current time in `time-zone`, drawn under the bars so it never crosses their text.

The header's previous, Today and next buttons and its Day | Week | Month switch update `date` and `view` and fire
`date-change` and `view-change` with `{ date, view, start, end }`, the moment to load that range's bookings.
`hide-header` drops the header for an app that drives the range from its own toolbar.

## Editing

`editable` lets users arrange bookings. The scheduler never changes `events` itself: it reports what the user asked
for, and you update `events`.

- **Create.** Dragging across empty time fires `range-select` with `{ resource, start, end }`, snapped to
  `snap-minutes` (15 by default). A click selects the column under the pointer.
- **Move and resize.** Dragging a bar moves it in time and to another row; dragging either edge resizes it. Both fire
  `event-change` with `{ event, start, end, resource }`. A dashed preview follows the pointer, and Escape cancels.
- **Read-only.** Bookings with `readonly: true` stay in place. `event-click` fires on any bar.

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
scheduler.addEventListener('event-change', ({ detail }) => {
  const { event, start, end, resource } = detail
  scheduler.events = scheduler.events.map((item) => (item === event ? { ...item, start, end, resource } : item))
})
scheduler.addEventListener('range-select', ({ detail }) => {
  scheduler.events = [...scheduler.events, { id: crypto.randomUUID(), title: 'New booking', ...detail }]
})
```

## Keyboard and screen readers

The grid is one Tab stop holding a slot cursor. The arrow keys move it over every slot of a row, booked or free, Up
and Down move it to the same slot of the next row, and Home and End jump to the first and last slot. A booked slot is
read with its bookings. Enter on a booked slot focuses its first bar; on a free slot, with `editable`, Enter fires
`range-select` for it. On a bar, with `editable`, Left and Right move it by `snap-minutes`, Shift+Left and Shift+Right
change its end, and Alt+Up and Alt+Down move it to the previous or next resource; Up and Down go to the next row and
Escape goes back to the slot cursor. Each bar is a button named by its title, resource and time ("Kick-off, Ana Ruiz,
Monday 9 November, 09:00 to 10:30"), and moves and new bookings are announced.

## Limits

Rows are not virtualised: the scheduler stays smooth up to about 200 resources and 2,000 bookings in view. Recurring
bookings are expanded by the app before they reach `events`.
