## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-tooltip`. Hover the trigger in a card to see it. The bubble is `--c2-tooltip--*` (colours, radius, border, shadow, font, padding, max-width, offset, animation) and the arrow is `--c2-tooltip__arrow--size`, or `hide-arrow`.
