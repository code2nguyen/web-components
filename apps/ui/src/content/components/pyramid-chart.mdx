A pyramid shows the parts of a whole in order: customer tiers, a test suite, headcount by level. `label-field`
names each level, a single `c2-chart-series` supplies its value, and one row is one level. It is drawn by
ECharts and, like the [pie chart](../pie-chart), has no axes, a per-level tooltip and a legend whose entries
highlight a level (or, with `legend-action="toggle"`, switch it off). Clicking a level highlights it too.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/chart/pyramid-chart'
```

This chart draws with [ECharts](https://echarts.apache.org), a peer dependency it loads on first draw: `npm install echarts`.

### Sizing

`sizing` decides what a level's size means, and it is the choice that matters most:

- `area` (the default) keeps the outline a triangle and gives each level a share of its area equal to its share
  of the total. It is the reading a pyramid invites, and small levels near the apex still get a visible band.
- `height` keeps the triangle and makes each level's height proportional to its value. It reads like a stacked
  bar; very small levels shrink to a sliver.
- `width` is the classic funnel: every level is the same height and its base is as wide as its value. The
  outline stops being a triangle, and the levels are always ordered by value.
- `equal` gives every level the same height inside a perfect triangle, so only the order is encoded and the
  values have to be read from the labels.

### Order and orientation

`sort="ascending"` (the default) puts the smallest value at the apex, `descending` the largest, and `none`
keeps the order of the rows, read from the apex. `apex` says where the point is: `top`, `bottom` for an
inverted pyramid that reads top to bottom as a narrowing process (the smallest stage still sits at the point), or `start` and `end` to lay it on its side (they follow the
writing direction).

```html tag=CodeBlock
<c2-pyramid-chart label-field="stage" apex="bottom" legend="none" data='[…]'>
  <c2-chart-series field="candidates" label="Candidates"></c2-chart-series>
</c2-pyramid-chart>
```

### Labels and styling

`labels` is `outside` by default, because the apex level is rarely wide enough to hold text; `inside` and
`none` are the alternatives. `label-content` picks `name`, `value`, `percent`, `name-value` (the default) or
`name-percent`. Outside labels that would collide near the apex are pushed apart, and an inside label that does
not fit its level is left to the tooltip.

The shape is styled through CSS variables on the host: `--c2-chart__level--border-radius` rounds every level's
corners, the apex included (`4px` by default, `0` for sharp corners), `--c2-chart__level--gap` spaces the levels apart,
`--c2-chart__level--min-width` gives the pyramid a flat top, `--c2-chart__level--max-width` narrows its base,
and `--c2-chart__level--align` (`start`, `center`, `end`) moves it across its axis. `--c2-chart__slice--border`
draws the edge between levels, as it does between pie slices. Level colours come from
`--c2-chart__series-1…8--color`, one per row.

Switching a level off from the legend, or with `setLevelVisible(label, false)`, lays the remaining levels out
again so they still fill the shape.
