## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-progress`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. The bar is two boxes: `--c2-progress--height` and `--c2-progress--border-radius` give it its shape, the indicator and track colours give it its tone, and `--c2-progress--animation-duration` sets the pace of both the sweep and the fill.
