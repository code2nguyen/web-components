`c2-quantity-picker` is the guests picker of Airbnb and Booking.com: a trigger that sums up several counts and opens a panel of labelled counters, each a `c2-quantity` with its own limits.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/quantity-picker'
```

## Usage

```html tag=UsageBlock,package=@c2n/quantity-picker
<style>
  .rooms {
    width: 320px;
    max-width: 100%;
  }
</style>
<div data-label="Popover, at most 6 guests">
  <c2-quantity-picker label="Guests" max-total="6">
    <c2-quantity name="adults" value="2" min="1" one="adult" other="adults">Adults<span slot="hint">Ages 13 or above</span></c2-quantity>
    <c2-quantity name="children" value="1" one="child" other="children">Children<span slot="hint">Ages 2–12</span></c2-quantity>
    <c2-quantity name="infants" one="infant" other="infants">Infants<span slot="hint">Under 2</span></c2-quantity>
  </c2-quantity-picker>
</div>
<div data-label="Inline, in a booking panel">
  <c2-quantity-picker class="rooms" label="Rooms" inline>
    <c2-quantity name="rooms" value="1" min="1" max="8" one="room" other="rooms">Rooms</c2-quantity>
    <c2-quantity name="guests" value="2" min="1" max="4" one="guest" other="guests">Guests per room</c2-quantity>
  </c2-quantity-picker>
</div>
```

### The summary

The trigger reads each counter's count with its noun, `one` or `other` as `Intl.PluralRules` picks, joined in the reader's language with `Intl.ListFormat`, and leaves out the counters at 0 ("2 adults, 1 child"). `placeholder` shows while every counter is 0, and `summary` replaces the whole text with a function of your own:

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
picker.summary = (counters) => `${counters.reduce((sum, counter) => sum + counter.value, 0)} guests`
```

### Limits and values

Each `c2-quantity` has `min`, `max` and `step`; `max-total` on the picker caps the sum, and at the limit every + is off and the note says so. `values` reads `{ adults: 2, children: 1, … }` by each counter's `name` (or its label in lower case), and `total` the sum. `input` fires on every step and `change` when the panel closes after a change (on every step when `inline`), both with `{ values, total }`. The picker is not form-associated: send `values` with your form.

### Keyboard and screen readers

The trigger's name includes the label ("Guests: 2 adults, 1 child"). The panel opens on the first counter, whose value is a spinbutton: ↑ and ↓ step it, Home and End jump to its limits. Escape, a click outside or Done closes the panel and returns focus to the trigger, and each change is announced with the new summary.
