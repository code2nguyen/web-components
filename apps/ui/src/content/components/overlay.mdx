`c2-overlay` is a positioning shell around the browser Popover API. It lives in the top layer, opens and light-dismisses natively (Escape, click outside, `popover="manual"` to opt out), and floating-ui keeps it next to its anchor: the trigger whose `commandfor` (or `popovertarget`) is the overlay's `id`, or whatever the `anchor` attribute (an id) or property (an element) points at. The preferred `placement` flips and shifts to stay on screen; the one in use is reflected as `current-placement`, and `--c2-overlay--available-height` / `--available-width` tell the content how much room it has. The overlay is at least as wide as its anchor unless `free-width` is set, and exactly as wide with `fit-anchor`. The slotted content brings its own surface. A `c2-button` opens it with `commandfor="<id>"` and `command="toggle-popover"` (or `show-popover`, `hide-popover`), which the overlay also runs itself in a browser without invoker commands. `c2-select` and `c2-color-select` are built on it.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/overlay'
```
