## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-save-bar`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. The `__container--*` variables restyle
the floating surface, `__indicator--*` the dot before the message, and `__discard--*` / `__save--*` the two buttons;
`__container--width: 100%` with a zero radius turns the floating bar into a full-width strip. Each card sits in a frame
standing in for the form the bar floats over.
