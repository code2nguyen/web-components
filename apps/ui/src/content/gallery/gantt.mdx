## Presets

Looks for project plans and roadmaps. The bar is drawn from one hue, `--c2-gantt__bar--color`, mixed against the
chart's background by `__bar--background-mix`, `__bar--border-mix` and `__progress--background-mix`; a task's `tone`
swaps the hue. Density comes from `__row--height` and `__bar--height`, and the zoom from the three
`__column-*--width` variables.
