## Presets

Curated looks for `c2-org-chart`. Open any card in the studio to start from it. The card is `--c2-org-chart__card--*` (`width`, `flex-direction` and `text-align` decide its shape), the lines are `__connector--*` (`border-radius` rounds the corners towards the outer reports, `border-style` dashes them), and `__level--gap` and `__sibling--gap` space the drawing. `__avatar--display: none` turns a chart of people into a chart of teams.
