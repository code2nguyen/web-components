`c2-menubar` is the row of menus at the top of a document or code editor: File, Edit, View. Each menu is a `c2-menubar-menu` with a `label` for its trigger, and its children are the rows of a `c2-menu`: `c2-menu-item` elements (checkbox and radio rows, shortcuts, descriptions, submenus), `<hr>` separators and `<h1>`–`<h6>` group headings.

The bar is one Tab stop. On a trigger, ArrowLeft and ArrowRight move across the bar, Home and End jump to the ends, and a letter jumps to the next menu that starts with it. Enter, Space or ArrowDown open the menu on its first row, and ArrowUp opens it on its last row. Inside a menu, ArrowRight opens a submenu or, on a plain row, moves to the next menu. ArrowLeft closes a submenu or, at the top level, moves to the previous menu. Escape closes the menu and returns focus to its trigger. With the pointer, a click opens a menu, and while one is open, hovering another trigger switches to it. On the triggers, right-to-left layouts swap the arrow keys; inside a menu they keep the meaning `c2-menu` gives them.

Picked rows fire `menu-select`, which bubbles through the bar, so one listener on `c2-menubar` handles the whole bar. The `start` and `end` slots hold what sits beside the menus, such as an app icon, a document title or a "Saved" status. They are outside the `menubar` role. `disabled` on a `c2-menubar-menu` greys out its trigger, and the arrow keys skip it.

Use a menubar for an app that has many commands, such as an editor or a design tool. For site navigation, use `c2-navigation-menu`. For a few actions, use a `c2-menu` behind a button.

## Import

```typescript tag=CodeBlock
import '@c2n/components/menubar'
```

Importing `@c2n/components/menubar` also registers `c2-menubar-menu`, `c2-menu` and `c2-menu-item`.
